Why this, not better Blender

AI collapsed the cost of making a still, a 10-second loop, and a mid-poly pack. It did not invent a buyer. CGTrader’s 2026 market report (marketplace data Jun 2025–May 2026) is the cleanest public stress test: AI-generated models were 17.6% of uploads and 24.1% in the latest month, but 2.6% of purchases and 1.1% of revenue — about one dollar in ninety. Twenty percent of CG buyers tried AI and found it not good enough; five percent said it works well. Print buyers are colder: about one in twenty-five say AI works well.

That is not “AI failed.” Purchase share of AI models more than tripled off a tiny base. It is the proof that SKU lock, materials, camera, and identity still clear the register. TSIA/Projectworks (Jan 2026) report clients already demanding 30–50% bid cuts “because AI,” with only about 3% of professional-services firms AI-mature.

Companies that look like $100M-class outcomes staff FDEs as a loss-leader for product. a16z’s 2025 framing: services sold at cost to buy a product moat. Bessemer: AI centaurs still average 5.7 years to $100M ARR. Accel’s Prayank Swaroop, Dec 2025 to TechCrunch: India still has no AI-first company at $40–50M revenue on a one-year clock. Sierra hit $100M ARR in 21 months by charging when the agent resolves.

The founder move is not “more render hours.” It is demand ownership plus a rubric the buyer cannot get from a $50 pack. If eight productize hours a week are forbidden by cash, intern-to-CEO is costume. Revisit 1 Apr 2027.

Founder body · brief subject ~40

Walking and a ten-hour window beat gadgets and a heroic gym.

Keep rosuvastatin as prescribed. 8–10k steps. 8–10 hour TRE. Deep-neck flexors daily. McKenzie only if it centralizes. No overhead press, no back squat, no barbell on the neck. Benfotiamine is not a liver drug. Subject of this tab is the brief’s ~40 founder, not a merged biography. BMI / CAP / MTHFR = Unknown. Judgment

Journal of Hepatology, Dec 2025 (Oh / Yoon / Jun, n=333 full analysis of 337 randomized): 16-week TRE cut MRI-PDFF −25.8% against standard of care +0.7% (p<0.001). Calorie restriction hit −24.7%. TRE ≈ CR on steatosis, weight, waist, and fat mass. Liver stiffness, glucose, and sleep were similar. No serious adverse events. The operational lesson is pick one window and stop switching.

Walking is the first lever that does not fight a cervical budget. Combined aerobic plus unloaded strength is the second. Rosuvastatin 10 mg for 52 weeks reduced intrahepatic lipid versus control in a non-diabetic MetS / MAFLD RCT (PubMed 39326342); a 2025 JAMA Internal Medicine cohort of 16,501 chronic liver disease patients associated statin use with lower 10-year HCC and decompensation. Do not stop a prescribed statin on internet folklore.

Cervical: Archives of PMR 2024 meta-analysis, 11 RCTs, 677 patients. McKenzie MDT vs control: pain −1.14/10 (p<0.02), NDI not significant. Moderate-to-severe pain −2.06. Use it only if symptoms centralize. Deep-neck flexor / CCFT work (Jull 2002 protocol) is the best-effort daily dose: 10×10s twice daily, 6–12 weeks. Collars, posture shirts, and buzzers stay on the kill list.

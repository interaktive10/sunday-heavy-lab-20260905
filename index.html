Founder body · brief subject ~40

Walk. Window. Neck. Keep the statin.

Keep rosuvastatin as prescribed. 8–10k steps. Eat inside an 8–10 hour window (TRE). Deep-neck flexors daily. McKenzie only if pain centralizes. No overhead press, no back squat, no bar on the neck. Subject is the brief’s ~40 founder, not a merged biography. BMI / CAP / MTHFR = Unknown. Judgment

TRE means time-restricted eating: all calories inside one 8–10 hour window, same window every day, stop switching. Journal of Hepatology Dec 2025 (n=333): 16-week TRE cut liver fat (MRI-PDFF) −25.8% against standard of care +0.7%. Calorie restriction hit −24.7%. Pick one. Walking is the first lever that does not fight a cervical budget. Combined aerobic plus unloaded strength is the second. Do not stop a prescribed statin on internet folklore.

Centralize, in McKenzie terms, means arm or shoulder pain moving toward the spine — a good sign. If it does not, skip McKenzie. Deep-neck flexor / CCFT (Jull 2002): 10 × 10-second holds, twice daily, 6–12 weeks. Collars, posture shirts, and buzzers stay dead.

California ordinary gains

Export the lots. Drip the expensive shares.

Do not sell until Book A and Book B lot reports exist as files. Then drip the high-basis lots (option B). If the export slips past day 14, pause (option C). California taxes all capital gains as ordinary income — there is no state discount for holding a year. NAV, lots, MAGI = Unknown. No VOO. No tickets. Judgment
